What the contract enforces

  • Only the provider can charge, and only the exact price.
  • At most one charge per interval (a relative timelock).
  • Every charge must carry the alert's sha256 fingerprint in OP_RETURN.
  • The rest goes straight back into your escrow.
  • You can refund everything at any time with your own signature. No one can stop it.
